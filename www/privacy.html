Privacy

GRAPPLE FROG · LAST UPDATED 29 SEPTEMBER 2026

The short version: Grapple Frog has no accounts, no ads and no trackers. It never asks for your name, email, age or location. The only thing that leaves your device is a leaderboard entry — a display name you choose yourself, plus your score — and a few anonymous counters telling me how the game is being played. If you want any of it removed, email me and it's gone.

What stays on your device

Most of the game is saved only on the device you're playing on, in your browser's local storage (or the app's own storage). It never leaves unless you post a score:

  • Flies you've banked, and which skins and tongue trails you own and have equipped
  • Your best scores, your best daily time, and which daily challenges you've finished
  • Whether sound is muted and which control scheme you picked
  • The display name you chose, so you don't have to type it again

Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of it. There's no backup and no way for me to restore it, so a fresh install starts from scratch.

What gets sent to the server

Two things, and only these two.

1. Leaderboard entries. When you finish or attempt a daily challenge, the game sends:

  • the display name you typed (2–12 letters or numbers — pick anything you like)
  • which daily it was, your time or distance, and how many flies you caught
  • your league's 6-character join code, if you're in a private league

If you create a private league, the league's name and its join code are stored too, so that anyone with the code can join it.

2. Anonymous play counters. To see whether people are enjoying the game, it records events like "a run started", "a daily was finished", "the shop was opened" or "a skin was bought", along with the mode and a single number (a distance, a time, or a price in flies).

Each of these carries a random ID like 3f9c2e18-7b44-4a91-b0d2-6e5c1a8f2d77, generated on your device the first time you play. That's all it is: a random number. It isn't linked to you, your device's real identifiers, or anything you've done anywhere else, and it's only there so two runs from the same player aren't counted as two different people. Clear your site data or reinstall and you get a brand new one.

Where it's kept

Scores, leagues and counters live in a Supabase database. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every website, those servers see the IP address your request came from as part of delivering the page — that's standard plumbing, I don't use it for anything, and it isn't joined up with your scores or your random ID.

What Grapple Frog doesn't do

  • No ads. None, anywhere, ever.
  • No third-party trackers or analytics SDKs. No Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no ad networks, no attribution kits. The counters above are homegrown and go straight to my own database.
  • No accounts, no passwords, no email addresses.
  • No personal data. Your real name, email, phone number, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone and location are never asked for, read or stored.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. There's nobody to sell it to and nothing worth selling.

Players under 18

Grapple Frog is suitable for all ages and anyone is welcome to play, including children. There's nothing age-restricted in it, and because the game never asks for personal information, it doesn't knowingly collect any from children or anyone else.

One thing worth knowing: the display name you enter appears on a public leaderboard that other players can see. If you're a young player — or a parent helping one — choose a nickname rather than a real full name. That name is the only thing on the leaderboard that you choose, so it's the only thing to be thoughtful about.

Deleting your data

You have two options, and neither needs an account.

  • Do it yourself. Clearing the site data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, wipes your local save and your random ID immediately. Any leaderboard scores already posted stay up under the display name you used.
  • Ask me. Email Wickus.myburgh@outlook.com with the display name you played under — and the league code, if you want a league removed — and I'll delete those entries. No proof or account needed; tell me the name and it goes. I'll confirm once it's done.

The anonymous counters can't be traced back to a person by design, so there's nothing identifiable in them to remove. If you'd like them cleared anyway and you still have your random ID, send it along and I'll delete those rows too.

Changes

If what the game collects ever changes, I'll update this page and change the date at the top. There's no mailing list to notify, because I don't have your email.

Questions

Anything at all — privacy, a deletion request, or just a bug — email Wickus.myburgh@outlook.com.
